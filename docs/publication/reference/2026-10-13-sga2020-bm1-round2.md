**Date:** 2026-10-13. **Isoster:** 1.1.1 (cubic ring read, renderer frame fix); fitted
with 1.1.1, residual metrics rescored with 1.1.2 on 2026-10-19 (issue 1 below; no refit).
**Campaign and analysis code:** the `sga_isoster` repository, branch
`feature/bm1r2-phase4-analysis` (not merged), `scripts/campaign/analysis/`

(diagnostics named below). **Reports:** `sga_isoster/output/qa/bm1r2_5batch_summary_v4.md`
and `sga_isoster/output/qa/bm1r2_phase4/*.md` (gitignored there; the numbers below are

## Question

The two round-2 mock campaigns (see the [combined summary](2026-10-11-round2-combined-summary.md))
measured accuracy against truth, and the [SGA-2020 spot check](2026-10-12-sga2020-cubic-spot-check.md)
tried the cubic default on twelve real cutouts. This record answers the questions only a
large real sample can:

1. Does isoster 1.1.1 run cleanly on every galaxy, and how does it compare with photutils
   and AutoProf on the same cutouts?
2. Real data have no truth. Which isoster features have a measurable advantage anyway,
   and for which kinds of galaxy? In particular, does an option that ranks low on
   average win clearly on some subset?
3. Which galaxies show each feature's advantage well enough to be shown in the paper
   (poster children)?
4. Does isoster reproduce the published SGA-2020 surface-brightness profiles?
5. What does one fit cost on real cutouts, one process, against the other two tools?
6. Do the multi-band joint fit and forced photometry give the same colours?

## Setup

- **Sample.** `benchmark_sample1_v2` of the SGA-2020 benchmark set: 1,999 galaxies, 999
  early and 1,000 late types (E 607, S0 392, Sa-Sb 346, Sbc-Sd 636, Sdm-Irr 18), r-band
  Legacy Survey cutouts, 0.262 arcsec per pixel, nanomaggies, with the inverse-variance
  maps and the campaign masks. R_ref is the SGA `MAJORAXIS` (median 85 px).
- **Runs.** Five batches of 400 galaxies (the last 399), 40 arms each: 30 isoster arms,
  5 photutils and 5 AutoProf arms; 79,960 fits at 16 parallel workers. Every isoster
  arm starts from the SGA ellipse (centre, ellipticity, position angle), with
  `maxsma` = half the cutout, the variance map `1/invvar`, twelve first-isophote
  retries, and `debug`, `full_photometry` and `compute_cog` on. Arms change one option
  (or one family of options) from `ref_default`, which is the 1.1.1 library default.
- **Measures (no truth).** For each fit, on pixels fixed per galaxy so that every arm and
  tool is judged on the same pixels:
  - *data residual*: mean |data − model| / sqrt(variance) in three elliptical zones at
    the catalogue geometry: inner < 0.5 R_ref, mid 0.5-1.5 R_ref, outer 1.5 R_ref to
    min(maxsma, 3 R_ref). Models are each tool's native render with harmonics on. Pure
    Gaussian noise reads 0.80 if the inverse variance is exact;
  - *outer wander*: the largest change of the centre, position angle (rings with
    ellipticity > 0.05) and ellipticity beyond R_ref, relative to the fit's own median
    geometry at 0.5-1 R_ref;
  - the fraction of rings with a non-zero stop code, the ring count and the fit time.
- **Thresholds from the data.** Two arm pairs should agree: `conv_no_lazy` (full
  gradient) and `samp_cubic_rms010` (kernel switch), each against `ref_default`. The 95 %
  quantile of their per-galaxy change sets the threshold for "better" and "worse":

  | measure | threshold |
  |---|---:|
  | inner residual (relative) | 10.0 % |
  | mid residual (relative) | 5.2 % |
  | outer residual (relative) | 1.0 % |
  | outer centre wander | 9.6 px |
  | outer PA wander | 25.3 deg |
  | outer ellipticity wander | 0.10 |
  | non-zero stop fraction | 0.046 |

  A *win* (or *loss*) is a galaxy where the feature arm beats (or trails) its baseline
  by more than the threshold. A null comparison reads about 2.5 % / 2.5 %.

## Results

### 1. Robustness

isoster finished all 59,970 of its fits without an error. photutils failed 220 of
9,995 (2.2 %: no isophote at any of the campaign's sma0 values, or an index error inside
photutils on an empty isophote list) and AutoProf 34 (0.3 %). The failures are on the same galaxies as in the previous run
of this benchmark (bm1 v3, isoster before 1.1.0).

### 2. The campaign's composite score does not rank fit quality

The campaign ranks arms by a composite of closeness to `ref_default` (weight 0.4), ring
count, centre stability and fit time (0.2 each), each min-max normalised over the arms.
In the top half of the table the first three inputs are identical: the median |dSB|
against `ref_default` is 0.0000 mag, the median ring count is 64, and the median centre
drift is 5.00 px, the step clip. The order of the top half therefore follows the
16-worker fit time (Spearman 0.80 between the composite rank and the fit-time rank over
the top 14 arms), which differs by hundredths of a second between arms and with the run
order. The ranking agrees with bm1 v3 (Spearman 0.94 over 27 shared arms), but
`ref_default`'s move from 12th to 2nd, and the "champions" of either run, reflect timing
noise. The composite cannot show an arm that fits better than the default, since it
rewards agreement with the default. Every statement about a feature below uses the
absolute measures instead.

Reading:

- **Harmonics are the strongest single feature on real galaxies.** The default orders 3-4
  improve the inner residual beyond the null on 58.8 % of galaxies and worsen it on
  0.2 %. Higher orders add more (orders 3-8: 76.1 % / 0.4 %), at the price of an
  outer-zone loss on 7-9 % of galaxies (presumably the extra terms fitting noise; not
  tested).
- **Variance weights protect against catastrophic fits.** Without them, the first three
  rings of 45 galaxies fail the gradient-error test (stop code −1: the ring-to-ring
  scatter, inflated by structure or a saturated neighbour, sets the error), the fit
  stops with `FIRST_FEW_ISOPHOTE_FAILURE`, and the inner residual exceeds the weighted
  fit's by more than 5 sigma; the reverse never happens. An earlier hypothesis that
  unmasked zero-weight pixels were responsible was tested and refuted (there were none,
  and masking them changed nothing). The price: the weighted fit ends more rings with a
  non-zero stop code on 25.4 % of galaxies (fewer on 0.7 %).
- **The first-isophote retry costs nothing and rescues 12 galaxies** that otherwise end
  with no or almost no rings.
- **Low rank, real wins.** Eccentric-anomaly sampling ranks 27th of 30, because it moves
  the profile away from the default. Yet on galaxies with b/a ≤ 0.55 it wins the inner
  zone on 48 % and loses on 13 %. Orders 3-8 rank 13th and win as above. Neither win is
  visible in the composite.
- **The automatic LSB lock acts on few galaxies and never hurts.** It reduces the outer
  wander beyond the null on 4-5 % of galaxies, increases it on none, and leaves the
  residual unchanged.
- **The outer regularization's onset is mis-scaled for these galaxies.** All three
  `reg_outer_*` arms freeze the outer geometry (wander 0 at the median) but lose the
  inner and mid zones on 76-85 % of galaxies: the onset (`outer_reg_sma_onset` =
  50 px, ramp 20 px, tuned on HSC data) sits inside an SGA galaxy's body (median R_ref
  85 px). The calibration should scale with the galaxy size, not a fixed pixel radius.
- **Options with no residual advantage on this sample:** the adaptive integrator,
  clipping variants, `geom_simul`, `conv_scale_sqrt`, `reg_central_on`, in-loop ISOFIT
  (after the 1.1.2 rescore, issue 1). Their residuals
  are within the null or worse (`permissive_on` loses the outer zone on 15 %,
  `step_1_2` the inner zone on 31 %). Two have a minor non-residual win: `geom_simul`
  ends fewer rings with a non-zero stop code (11.1 / 1.9; 24.4 / 1.9 for galaxies
  fainter than r = 15.2), and `sclip_asym_32` steadies the outer centre (6.1 / 0.6).

### 4. Cubic ring read on 1,999 galaxies

Cubic `ref_default` against bilinear `samp_bilinear`, paired per galaxy:

| class | n | inner residual cubic / bilinear (median) | galaxies where cubic is better | ring dI / error (median) | ring dI (median) |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| all | 1999 | 0.968 / 0.978 | 1,333 | +2.77 | +0.51 % |
| early | 999 | 0.697 / 0.708 | 829 | +3.45 | +0.51 % |
| late | 1000 | 1.671 / 1.666 | 504 | +2.24 | +0.51 % |

Ring dI is cubic minus bilinear in the inner quarter of D25/2. In the mid and outer
zones the two reads agree within the errors (residual change 0.000-0.002, ring change
below 0.05 sigma), although the faint outer rings differ by −1.1 % at the median (−2.1 %
on late types).
The real-data result matches the mocks and the spot check: the cubic read makes cores
0.5 % brighter, significant ring by ring, and the cubic model describes the pixels
better on 83 % of early types. On late types the inner residual is dominated by
structure the ellipses cannot follow, and the gain disappears. The gain per galaxy is
about 1 %, below the 10 % null threshold of section 3, so it is a statistical claim and
no single galaxy shows it well (section 10). The cubic read also ends fewer rings with a
non-zero stop code (better on 16.7 % of galaxies, worse on 5.5 %).

The kernel switch (`samp_cubic_rms010`) fires on every galaxy, at 0.25-0.77 R_ref
(p10-p90): on Legacy Survey depth the rings become noise-dominated inside the galaxy
body. It is indistinguishable from the default on every measure (it is one of the null
pairs).

### 5. Against photutils and AutoProf, and the renderer

Default arm of each tool, 1,962 galaxies that all three fit; win = isoster lower by more
than 5 %.

| zone | isoster p50 | photutils p50 | AutoProf p50 | isoster better / worse than photutils | isoster better / worse than AutoProf |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| inner | 0.963 | 0.898 | 1.491 | 23 % / 42 % | 85 % / 10 % |
| mid | 0.752 | 0.748 | 0.785 | 15 % / 19 % | 47 % / 6 % |
| outer | 0.724 | 0.730 | 0.726 | 12 % / 1 % | 3 % / 1 % |

isoster beats AutoProf in the inner zone on 85 % of galaxies (97 % of ellipticals). It
trails photutils in the inner zone on spirals (worse on 65-67 % of Sa-Sd), and the
cause is **isoster's renderer, not its fit**
(`sga_isoster/scripts/onetime/_qa_inner_resid_fit_vs_render.py`, 60 galaxies per
subset). The same photutils fit drawn by photutils' renderer reads 1.45 sigma in the
inner zone of random spirals and 2.05 sigma drawn by isoster's (worse on 85 % of them;
2.57 with harmonics off). On ellipticals both renderers give 0.70. Drawn by one
renderer, the isoster fit beats the photutils fit in the inner zone on 48 % of spirals
(loses on 20 %) and on 52 % of ellipticals (loses on 2 %). The mechanism in isoster's
renderer is not yet identified. Consequence for the paper: a cross-tool residual
comparison must draw every tool's fit with one renderer.

**Noise units depend on the survey footprint.** North of Dec 32.375 deg (BASS r band)
the inverse-variance maps overstate the pixel noise about twofold: the sky-dominated
outer zone reads 0.39 north (489 galaxies) and 0.74 south (1,473) for all three tools
alike. Noise-unit levels must be quoted per footprint; paired comparisons on one galaxy
are unaffected.

### 6. Outskirts and depth

Outer wander of the default free fit (median): centre 6.3 px, PA 10.1 deg, ellipticity
0.089. It is not reproducible at this level: the two null pairs move the outer centre by
up to 9.6 px and the outer PA by up to 25 deg (95 % of galaxies). The free outer geometry
of a Legacy Survey galaxy beyond R_ref is noise-dominated. Locking it (LSB lock) or
regularizing it is the right idea, but the regularization onset must sit beyond the
body (section 3).

Every isoster arm stops at the same radius, half the cutout (2.38 R_ref at the median),
so depth cannot separate isoster arms in this campaign. photutils stops at the same
radius, while AutoProf goes to 2.76 R_ref (3.07 with its `deep` arm). The faintest
surface brightness reached: isoster 29.47, photutils 29.57, AutoProf 29.97 mag/arcsec²
(medians).

| ratio, per galaxy (20 galaxies) | p10 | median | p90 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

The integrator choice does not drive the ratio: photutils' default costs about the same
as its median arm. The difference between 112 here and 162 above is the sample (20
against 100 galaxies, a ratio that grows with galaxy size), so on SGA cutouts photutils
is about 100 to 160 times slower than isoster per fit at the median, with a tail beyond
300 on the largest galaxies.

The cubic read costs 10 % of a fit at the median here (p10-p90 −4 to +21 %), against
1-3 % in the mock campaigns and 3 % summed over the twelve-galaxy spot check; the
difference has not been investigated. The lazy gradient saves 20 % of a fit (the full gradient
costs 25 % more) with no residual change beyond the null.

## Issues found in isoster

1. **In-loop ISOFIT kept harmonics on failed rings (fixed in 1.1.2, 2026-10-19).** With
   `simultaneous_harmonics` and `isofit_mode="in_loop"` (`harm_isofit_inloop`,
   `harm_simul_ea`), 1.1.1 stored the joint-fit harmonic coefficients on rings that then
   failed (stop code −1: the gradient was not measurable), where every other path stores
   zero; divided by that gradient they were large (median amplitude 0.21 on the twelve
   spot-check galaxies), and the renderer drew them (outer residual 53-670 sigma on most
   of them, instead of about 1). 1.1.2 stores zero there, and the renderer now ignores the harmonics of
   any ring whose summed amplitude Σₙ √(aₙ² + bₙ²) is ≥ 1, since such a ring can push the
   corrected radius to zero. The fitter never reads stored harmonics back, so every
   isoster model of the campaign was re-rendered from its profile under the 1.1.2 rule
   and the metrics refreshed (no refit; 5,713 of 55,972 models changed, and metrics
   changed only on those rows). Against `ref_default`:

   | arm | inner | mid | outer |
   |---|---|---|---|

   few points of the null in every zone and wins clearly in no stratum; `harm_simul_ea` behaves like

   converge with a noise-level gradient where the geometry is locked or the fit continues
   past the gradient test, which is why `permissive_on` and `stack_all` moved (146 and 136
   of 400 batch-01 models changed, against at most 9 for the other arms). The earlier
   100-galaxy estimate (mid zone still worse on 16-17 %) was pessimistic; the full rescore
   gives 4-6 %. Record of the rescore: `sga_isoster/docs/plan/step5e_phase4_analysis.md`,

2. **The renderer is worse than photutils' on spiral cores** (section 5). The same fit
   reads 2.05 sigma through isoster's renderer and 1.45 through photutils' in the inner
   zone of random spirals. The ellipticals are unaffected. The mechanism is not yet
   identified; the first thing to test is how pixels are assigned to rings where
   consecutive inner rings twist or cross.
3. **Misleading `FIRST_FEW_ISOPHOTE_FAILURE` warning.** The message always ends "or
   enabling max_retry_first_isophote" (`isoster/driver.py`, the warning after the
   probe loop), also when the retries are enabled and have all run (twelve here). It
   should name the retries tried and suggest them only when `max_retry_first_isophote`
   is 0.
4. **Comparison figure** (`plot_comparison_qa_figure`), minor. The PA panel can drop
   an arm at a 180 deg wrap. Tick labels of stacked panels collide on some galaxies. The
   residual panels have no shared colour scale, show masked pixels (the mask is drawn on
   the data panel only) and always carry isophote overlays; the poster script patches
   the figure afterwards.

## What this means for the paper and the code

1. Real data support the 1.1.x defaults. The cubic read, harmonics 3-4, variance
   weights, the first-isophote retry and the lazy gradient each win or are neutral on
   the residual, and none costs more than its fit time, with one exception: the
   variance weights end more rings with a non-zero stop code (on 25.4 % of galaxies). Harmonics are the largest effect
   (inner residual better on 58.8 % of galaxies), and the variance weights and the
   retry prevent the catastrophic fits (45 and 12 galaxies).
2. isoster runs on every one of 1,999 real galaxies without an error, with a 0.11 s
   median per fit, 100 to 160 times faster than photutils (with its own default or its
   median integrator alike) and 44 times faster than AutoProf without its launch, on the
   same cutouts.
3. Advertise eccentric-anomaly sampling for elongated galaxies (b/a ≤ 0.55) and higher
   harmonic orders for disks and bars, with the outer-zone caveat. Both are invisible in
   an average ranking.
4. Do not use a composite score that rewards agreement with the default to rank options.
   Judge features per galaxy on absolute measures, with thresholds set by arm pairs that
   should agree.
5. The outer regularization's onset needs a size-scaled default (for example a multiple
   of R_ref or of the radius where the rings become noise-dominated) before it is
   advertised for nearby galaxies. The automatic LSB lock is the safer default for this
   kind of data.
6. Cross-tool residual comparisons in the paper must use one renderer, and the isoster
   renderer's spiral-core deficit (issue 2) should be fixed or explained first.
7. Quote noise-unit residual levels per survey footprint.
8. isoster reproduces the published SGA-2020 profiles on SGA's own ellipses to 0.01 mag
   at the median inside MAJORAXIS (p16-p84 −0.05 to +0.07), and the joint multi-band
   fit gives the same colours as forced photometry inside MAJORAXIS (0.005 mag) at 1.46
   times the cost.
